# Stakeholder Validation Report

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DISCLAIMER**: The user validation results presented in this report represent a **Simulated stakeholder validation** conducted with simulated rural clinic staff roles (Community Health Worker, Clinic Nurse, Medical Officer, Rural Health Inspector).

---

## 1. Evaluation Methodology
A 7-question survey using a **1–5 Likert Scale** (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree) was administered to 20 simulated healthcare participants across 4 clinical roles:

* Community Health Workers (N = 6)
* Staff Nurses (N = 6)
* Rural Medical Officers (N = 5)
* Health Inspectors (N = 3)

---

## 2. Questionnaire & Scores

| # | Survey Statement | Mean Score (1-5) | Standard Dev |
|---|------------------|------------------|--------------|

| Q3 | The clarification suggestion is useful. | 4.70 | 0.46 |
| Q4 | The escalation warning is clear. | 4.85 | 0.36 |
| Q5 | Human confirmation is appropriate. | 4.90 | 0.30 |
| Q6 | The uncertainty message is understandable. | 4.60 | 0.50 |

---

## 3. Aggregate Category Index Calculations

* **Average Clarity Score** (Q1, Q2, Q6): **4.60 / 5.00**
* **Average Usefulness Score** (Q3, Q7): **4.73 / 5.00**
* **Average Trust Score** (Q4, Q5): **4.88 / 5.00**
* **Overall Average Score**: **4.71 / 5.00** (94.2% Satisfaction)
